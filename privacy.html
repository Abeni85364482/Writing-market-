ARITI MARKET — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

ARITI MARKET is a marketplace that allows people to browse listings, create accounts, publish products and contact sellers.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use the service, we may process the email address and account identifier used to sign in, profile details you provide, product descriptions and prices, images you upload, and reports or messages you submit about listings.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, display marketplace listings, store uploaded product images, support seller-buyer contact, review reported listings, and maintain the security and operation of the service.

Storage and service providers

Account, listing and report information is stored using Supabase services. Product images are stored using Supabase Storage. These providers process information to operate the service.

Sharing

Marketplace listings and product images you choose to publish may be visible to other visitors. Seller contact details that you choose to display may be used by buyers to contact you. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion requests

Information is retained while needed to operate the service and address safety or legal obligations. To ask about access, correction or deletion of your account information, contact bekikeya@gmail.com from your registered email address. Some information may need to be retained where required for security or legal reasons.

Children

ARITI MARKET is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Security

We use access controls provided by our backend service and take reasonable steps to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Privacy questions: bekikeya@gmail.com
