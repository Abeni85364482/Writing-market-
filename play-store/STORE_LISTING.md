## Short description

## Full description
ARITI MARKET helps buyers and sellers discover and share products in one marketplace.

Features:
- Browse product listings and categories.
- Search for products.
- Create an account and publish product listings.
- Add product photos, descriptions and prices.
- Contact sellers through the contact details they choose to provide.
- Promote listings through the marketplace promotion feature.
- Report suspicious or inaccurate listings.

ARITI MARKET is a marketplace platform. Buyers should verify products and sellers before making payments.
